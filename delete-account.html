Delete your account and data

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your TicketCket account and the personal data associated with it.

1Before you start

TicketCket accounts are issued by the event organiser you work for. Deleting your account removes your access to every event assigned to you, and it cannot be undone. If you only want to stop using the app on one device, signing out is enough — that clears all stored data from that device.

We confirm every request from the account's registered email address before acting on it.

2What is deleted, and what is kept

DataOutcome
Your account and login credentials Deleted within 30 days of a verified request
Your profile details (name, email, role) Deleted within 30 days
Session and refresh tokens Revoked immediately; device copies cleared on sign-out or uninstall
Your app preferences and cached images Deleted from the device on uninstall
Scan and validation records tied to your account Anonymised — the ticket/order record remains for the organiser's event audit trail, but is no longer linked to you
Attendee and order data belonging to the organiser Retained by the organiser — it is their data, not yours, and we hold it on their behalf
Security and transaction logs Retained up to 90 days for fraud prevention and security, then deleted

4What happens next

  • We acknowledge your request within 5 business days.
  • We verify it came from the account's registered email address.
  • If your organiser must approve the removal of a staff account, we tell you and coordinate it.
  • Deletion completes within 30 days of verification, and we email you to confirm.

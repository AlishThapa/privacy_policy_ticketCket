# Privacy Policy — TicketCket (scan4Ticket)

**Last updated:** 17 October 2026
**Applies to:** the TicketCket mobile application for Android and iOS ("the App")

TicketCket ("we", "us", "our") provides an event ticket-scanning application used by
event staff and organisers to validate attendee tickets, view events, and look up
orders. This policy explains what the App collects, why, and what control you have
over it.

By using the App you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Who this App is for

The App is a **staff and organiser tool**, not a consumer ticket wallet. You need an
account issued by an event organiser to sign in. Attendees do not create accounts in
the App.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information you provide

When you sign in, reset your password, or verify a one-time password (OTP), we process:

- your email address or username
- your password (sent over an encrypted connection and never stored on the device in
  readable form)
- the OTP code sent to you during password recovery
- your profile details as held by your organiser — for example name, role, and contact
  email — which the App displays read-only

### 2.2 Ticket and order data

When you scan a ticket or search for an order, the App sends the scanned code or your
search terms to our servers and displays the result. This may include attendee names,
order references, ticket types, and event details belonging to your organiser. This
data is processed on behalf of your organiser; we do not use it for any other purpose.

### 2.3 Camera data

The App requests **camera** permission solely to read QR codes on tickets.

- Camera frames are processed **on your device**, in memory, for the sole purpose of
  decoding a QR code.
- We do **not** record video, take photographs, save images to your device or gallery,
  or transmit camera imagery to our servers or to anyone else.
- Only the decoded ticket code — a short string of text — is sent for validation.
- You can decline or revoke camera permission in your device settings at any time. The
  scanner will not work without it; the rest of the App will.

### 2.4 Technical information

To operate and secure the service, our servers receive standard request information
such as IP address, request timestamps, and the App version. The App also reads your
**network connection state** so it can tell you when you are offline; this check stays
on the device.

### 2.5 What we do NOT collect

The App does **not** collect or access:

- your location, GPS, or precise/approximate device position
- your contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, or messages
- your photo library, files, or microphone
- advertising identifiers; the App contains **no advertising and no third-party
  analytics, tracking, or profiling SDKs**

We do **not** sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it to build
advertising profiles or to train machine-learning models.

---

## 3. Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device using the operating
system's private app storage:

| Stored item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Authentication token | Keeps you signed in between sessions |
| Refresh token | Renews your session without re-entering your password |
| User ID | Associates the session with your account |
| App preferences | Remembers basic in-app settings |
| Cached images | Speeds up loading of event images |

This data is readable only by the App. **Signing out, or uninstalling the App, removes
it.** It is not shared with other apps.

---

## 4. How we use your information

We use the information described above only to:

1. authenticate you and keep your session secure;
2. validate tickets and show scan results;
3. display events, orders, and your profile;
4. send password-reset and OTP messages you request;
5. diagnose faults, prevent fraudulent or duplicate ticket use, and keep the service
   secure;
6. comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that affects your legal
rights.

---

## 5. How information is shared

- **With your event organiser.** Scan and order activity is visible to the organiser
  whose events you work on. For that organiser's data, we act as a processor and they
  act as the controller.
- **With service providers.** Our hosting and email/SMS delivery providers process data
  strictly on our instructions and under contract.
- **For legal reasons.** We may disclose information where required by law, court order,
  or to protect our rights, users, or the safety of others.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 6. Data transmission and security

- All communication between the App and our servers at `ticketcket.com` uses **HTTPS
  (TLS) encryption**.
- Credentials are transmitted only over that encrypted channel and are not written to
  logs.
- Session tokens are held in the operating system's sandboxed app storage.
- Access to production data is limited to authorised personnel.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will
notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

---

## 7. Data retention

- **Session tokens** on your device last until you sign out, they expire, or you
  uninstall the App.
- **Account and scan records** are retained by your event organiser for as long as
  needed for event operations, auditing, and dispute resolution, and then deleted or
  anonymised according to their retention schedule.
- **Server logs** are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

---

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct inaccurate information;
- request deletion of your account and associated data;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- receive a copy of your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent to device permissions (via your device settings).

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in section 12. Because much of
the data is held on behalf of an event organiser, we may need to direct your request to
that organiser, and we will tell you if so. We respond within the period required by
applicable law.

---

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for event staff aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children,
and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a
child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 10. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in countries other than your own.
Where personal information is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate
safeguards as required by applicable data-protection law.

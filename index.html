TicketCket ("we", "us", "our") provides an event ticket-scanning application used by event staff and organisers to validate attendee tickets, view events, and look up orders. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1Who this app is for

The app is a staff and organiser tool, not a consumer ticket wallet. You need an account issued by an event organiser to sign in. Attendees do not create accounts in the app.

2Information we collect

Account information you provide

When you sign in, reset your password, or verify a one-time password (OTP), we process:

  • your email address or username;
  • your password, sent over an encrypted connection and never stored on the device in readable form;
  • the OTP code sent to you during password recovery;
  • your profile details as held by your organiser — name, role, contact email — which the app displays read-only.

Ticket and order data

When you scan a ticket or search for an order, the app sends the scanned code or your search terms to our servers and displays the result. This may include attendee names, order references, ticket types, and event details belonging to your organiser. We process this data on their behalf and for no other purpose.

Camera data

The app requests camera permission solely to read QR codes on tickets. Camera frames are processed on your device, in memory, only to decode a QR code. We do not record video, take photographs, save images to your device or gallery, or transmit camera imagery anywhere. Only the decoded ticket code — a short string of text — is sent for validation.

You can decline or revoke camera permission in your device settings at any time. The scanner will not work without it; the rest of the app will.

Technical information

To operate and secure the service, our servers receive standard request information such as IP address, request timestamps, and the app version. The app also reads your network connection state so it can tell you when you are offline; that check stays on the device.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect or access:

  • your location, GPS, or precise/approximate device position;
  • your contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, or messages;
  • your photo library, files, or microphone;
  • advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, tracking, or profiling SDKs.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles or to train machine-learning models.

4How we use your information

We use the information described above only to:

  1. authenticate you and keep your session secure;
  2. validate tickets and show scan results;
  3. display events, orders, and your profile;
  4. send password-reset and OTP messages you request;
  5. diagnose faults, prevent fraudulent or duplicate ticket use, and keep the service secure;
  6. comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that affects your legal rights.

5How information is shared

  • With your event organiser. Scan and order activity is visible to the organiser whose events you work on. For that organiser's data we act as a processor and they act as the controller.
  • With service providers. Our hosting and email/SMS delivery providers process data strictly on our instructions and under contract.
  • For legal reasons. We may disclose information where required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, our users, or the safety of others.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

6Data transmission and security

  • All communication between the app and our servers at ticketcket.com uses HTTPS (TLS) encryption.
  • Credentials are transmitted only over that encrypted channel and are not written to logs.
  • Session tokens are held in the operating system's sandboxed app storage.
  • Access to production data is limited to authorised personnel.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

7Data retention

  • Session tokens on your device last until you sign out, they expire, or you uninstall the app.
  • Account and scan records are retained by your event organiser for as long as needed for event operations, auditing, and dispute resolution, then deleted or anonymised according to their retention schedule.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of your account and associated data, object to or restrict certain processing, receive a copy of your data in a portable format, and withdraw consent to device permissions via your device settings.

Because much of the data is held on behalf of an event organiser, we may need to direct your request to that organiser, and we will tell you if so. We respond within the period required by applicable law.

9Children's privacy

The app is intended for event staff aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in countries other than your own. Where personal information is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data-protection law.
